# Demo video script (under 90 seconds)

Record at 1920x1080 (crop to the overlay + Hermes window). One take, no cuts
needed — rehearse once. Post on X tagging @NousResearch, keep it under 90s.

| Time | Shot | Say / show |
|------|------|------------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Desktop with Kin floating over it; move the mouse around — the character's eyes follow the real cursor. | "This is Kin — a companion for Hermes Desktop. She floats over your desktop and watches your work." |
| 0:08–0:22 | Point at the drawer: RUNNING section — "Launch Titanfall 2 VR mod — 5 background processes, 12 hours". Click the row; Hermes Desktop focuses that session. | "The task drawer is plain English: what's running, what it's doing, for how long. Click a row to jump to that session." |
| 0:22–0:34 | Tap the mic button. The pill turns live, EQ bars move. Say: "What's the status of my Steam mod?" — the transcript lands in the Hermes composer as a draft. Show reviewing and pressing send. | "Voice is opt-in, and it never auto-sends — your words arrive as a draft you review." |
| 0:34–0:48 | Focus a terminal with an error on screen; hit the Term icon. Kin OCRs it, sends it to Hermes; show Hermes diagnosing. | "Snap and Term ask Hermes about your screen — capture, OCR, diagnose." |
| 0:48–1:00 | Switch persona (right-click the persona pill) — a different 3D bust swaps in and speaks a recap aloud. Meanwhile show the Hermes pane with the head reacting to a tool completing. | "Six personas, real moods — Kin reacts when a turn finishes, fails, or loops three times on the same call." |
| 1:00–1:10 | Close on the Hermes Desktop Plugins settings page: Kin enabled. End card: repo URL. | "Plugin plus optional overlay, MIT, in the Hermes plugin catalog. Kin." |

## Tips

- Quiet hours: mute the overlay's mic when not on the voice shot (it is opt-in — it will be off by default).
- If the drawer has many rows, collapse to the RUNNING section before recording.
- The greeting line and recap use the configured OpenAI TTS voice — test audio once before recording.
